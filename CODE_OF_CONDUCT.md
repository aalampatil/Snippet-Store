# Code of Conduct

Snippet Store welcomes participants of every background, identity, ability, and
experience level. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, other
project spaces, and anyone representing the project publicly.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and patience, especially newcomers.
- Give constructive feedback about ideas and code. Accept corrections and take
  responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect personal boundaries, accessibility needs, and privacy.
- Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention, personal attacks,
  deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without permission are
  unacceptable.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to the maintainer, [@aalampatil](https://github.com/aalampatil),
at [aalam.workacc@gmail.com](mailto:aalam.workacc@gmail.com). Include relevant links,
what happened, and any immediate concerns. Avoid posting sensitive details in
public issues. Reports will be reviewed fairly, and identifying information will
be shared only as needed to investigate and respond.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove harmful content and request a correction, issue a
warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently exclude someone,
depending on the severity and repetition of the behavior. Serious threats or
harassment may lead to immediate exclusion. Moderation decisions will be
explained to those affected when appropriate. Request reconsideration through
the same private contact, including any additional context.
